Advisory group members

Advisory group members are homeschool parents and other homeschool stakeholders from across Arkansas. If you have feedback about the EFA program, you can contact a member in or near your county, and they can bring it to the group's meetings.

Members cannot answer questions about your own EFA account or resolve account issues. For account questions, please contact the EFA program at help@schoolchoicear.org.
