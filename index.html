EFA Homeschool Advisory Group

About the group Meetings and summaries

A place for homeschool families to learn how the EFA program works and to be heard

The EFA Homeschool Advisory Group brings together homeschool parents and others who support homeschool families in the EFA program. Members meet with ADE staff to learn how the program is administered, share the needs and concerns they hear from other families, and take what they learn back to their homeschool communities.

What the group does and does not do

The group does

  • Learn how ADE administers the EFA program, including upcoming process changes
  • Share feedback on what is working and what concerns homeschool families
  • Pass along information from meetings to other homeschool families

The group does not

  • Vote, make decisions or set policy
  • Change the EFA law or program rules
  • Review or resolve individual family accounts

Who can apply

  • Parents or guardians of students who participate in the EFA program as homeschool students
  • Leaders or representatives of homeschool co-ops, microschools, learning pods or homeschool organizations
  • Other homeschool stakeholders

ADE will look for applicants who are connected to other homeschool families and can share information with them.

What members commit to

  • Meetings: four in-person meetings a year, one each quarter. ADE may schedule additional virtual meetings if needed.
  • Attendance: members are expected to attend all four in-person meetings, with one excused absence allowed each year.
  • Term: 1 year or 2 years, the member's choice.
  • Volunteer service: members receive no pay and no expense support for travel or other costs.
  • Participation agreement: selected members sign a short agreement that explains the role.

How to apply

  1. Complete the online application by Tuesday, December 1, 2026.
  2. ADE reviews applications and selects members.
  3. ADE notifies all applicants of its decision.

Please do not include student names, dates of birth or EFA account numbers in your application.
